Leonida Cover Lab turns any photo and a caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art with a Vice City neon-sunset grade, exported as a PNG to share — offline, in the browser, with the photos never leaving the machine. Drop in one photo or several, drag the panels to reframe them, switch between the Hero Grid, Duo Split and Triple Strip layouts, letter your own title and caption, and download the poster at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1600 × 900.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
